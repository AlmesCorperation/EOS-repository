## Preamble
The **Extended GNU Affero General Public License (EAGPL)** is an **Extended Open Source (EOS)** license. It builds upon the principles of traditional strong copyleft by closing loopholes regarding unmodified code copying and commercialization. 

The EAGPL ensures absolute reciprocity: if you benefit from the creator's labor by copying their code, you must give back to the community.
